# RicozSpark

> Enterprise Innovation & Idea Management Platform

RicozSpark is an enterprise innovation platform designed to transform employee ideas into measurable business outcomes.

It provides a structured workflow for employees to submit and discover ideas, enables reviewers and managers to evaluate and approve them, and supports the transition of approved ideas into projects that can be executed, measured, and tracked.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Organizations often receive valuable ideas from employees, but many ideas get lost because there is no structured process to capture, evaluate, execute, and measure them.

RicozSpark addresses this problem by providing a centralized innovation lifecycle:

**CAPTURE → DISCOVER → DISCUSS → COLLABORATE → EVALUATE → DECIDE → PLAN → EXECUTE → MEASURE → LEARN**

The platform connects the complete journey of an innovation idea — from its initial submission to its eventual business impact.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

RicozSpark aims to:

- Provide a centralized platform for employee ideas
- Make innovation discoverable across the organization
- Enable structured idea evaluation
- Support approval and decision workflows
- Convert approved ideas into executable projects
- Track projects, tasks, milestones, risks, and outcomes
- Measure the business impact of implemented ideas
- Provide leadership with innovation analytics
- Maintain role-based access and accountability
